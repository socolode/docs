# Product Management

Create, organize, and manage your product catalog.

## Adding a product

1. Go to **Products > Add Product**
2. Enter a **title** and **description** (supports rich text and HTML)
3. Upload **images** by dragging files into the upload area
4. Set the **price** and optional **compare-at price** (shows a discount)
5. Enter **SKU** and **barcode** for inventory tracking
6. Choose a **category** and **tags** for filtering

### Product variants

If a product comes in multiple sizes or colors, enable **Variants**:

- Click **Add Variant Option** and name it (e.g., "Size", "Color")
- Enter the available values (e.g., "S, M, L, XL")
- Set individual prices, SKUs, and stock levels per variant

### Inventory tracking

Each product and variant tracks stock independently. When stock reaches zero, the product is automatically marked as sold out unless you enable **Continue selling when out of stock**.

## Organizing products

### Collections

Collections group products for display on your storefront. There are two types:

- **Manual collections**: You select which products to include
- **Smart collections**: Products are added automatically based on rules (e.g., "Price > $50" or "Tag = summer")

### Categories and tags

- **Categories** form the main navigation of your store. Create a hierarchy (e.g., Women > Shoes > Sneakers)
- **Tags** are flexible labels for cross-cutting themes (e.g., "sale", "new-arrival", "eco-friendly")

## Bulk operations

### CSV import

Upload multiple products at once via **Products > Import**. Download the [CSV template](https://socolode.com/templates/products.csv) and fill in the required columns: `title`, `price`, `sku`, `inventory_quantity`, `category`.

### Bulk editing

On the product list page, select multiple products and use **Bulk Actions** to:

- Update prices by percentage or fixed amount
- Add or remove tags
- Change category
- Set status to active or draft

## Product status

| Status | Behavior |
|--------|----------|
| Active | Visible on storefront, purchasable |
| Draft | Hidden from storefront, editable |
| Archived | Hidden, not editable, excluded from reports |
