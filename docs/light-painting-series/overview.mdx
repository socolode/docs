# Light Painting Series

Welcome to Socolode's Light Painting Series — a collection of modular, easy-to-use light painting tools designed for photographers, creators, and hobbyists.

## What is Light Painting?

Light painting is a photographic technique where you create images by moving a light source during a long exposure. With Socolode's light painting wands, you can:

- **Paint with light** — Create letters, patterns, and artistic effects in long-exposure photos
- **Shoot portraits** — Add creative light trails and effects to portrait photography
- **Design custom patterns** — Upload your own images, text, or logos for unique light painting
- **Explore your creativity** — No drawing skills needed, just point, shoot, and create
