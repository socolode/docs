## FAQ

### What's the difference between OpenStick T1 and S1?

T1 and S1 are different models of light painting wands. Please refer to their respective product documentation for details. Documentation is being updated.

### What image formats does the light painting wand support?

Light painting wands typically support BMP format. You can use the [online tool](../light-painting-series/online-tool) to convert regular images to BMP format.

### How do I upload images to the light painting wand?

Connect the wand to your computer via USB, then copy BMP files to the image folder on the SD card or internal storage.

### Can I program the light painting wand myself?

The OpenStick series supports secondary development. You can write custom firmware and effects using Arduino IDE or other tools.

### Are accessories compatible across different wand models?

Some accessories are universal, while others are model-specific. Please refer to the compatibility notes in the accessory documentation.
