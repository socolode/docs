# Quick Start

Get your Socolode store live in five steps.

## 1. Create an account

Sign up at [socolode.com](https://socolode.com) with your email address. You will receive a verification email, click the link to activate your account.

## 2. Choose a plan

After activation, select a subscription plan that fits your business size. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from the dashboard.

| Plan | Products | Storage | Custom Domain |
|------|----------|---------|---------------|
| Starter | 50 | 2 GB | No |
| Growth | 500 | 20 GB | Yes |
| Pro | Unlimited | 100 GB | Yes |

## 3. Set up your store

Navigate to **Settings > Store Profile** and fill in:

- Store name and logo
- Contact email and phone
- Default currency and timezone
- Store description (used for SEO)

## 4. Add your first product

Go to **Products > Add Product** and enter:

1. Product title and description
2. Price and compare-at price
3. Images (drag and drop, up to 10 per product)
4. Inventory quantity and SKU
5. Shipping weight and category

Click **Publish** to make the product visible on your storefront.

## 5. Launch your store

Review your store preview at `https://your-store.socolode.com`. When you are satisfied:

1. Go to **Settings > Domains**
2. Connect a custom domain (Growth plan and above) or use the default subdomain
3. Click **Publish** to take your store live

Congratulations, your store is now online.
