# Beginner FAQ - Check Here First When You Have Issues

> 90% of beginner questions are solved here — no need to contact support

---

## 📸 Shooting Questions

**Q: Why are my photos completely black?**
A: 99% of the time it's because the environment isn't dark enough, or the shutter speed is too short. Find a completely dark place, turn off the lights, and set your phone's shutter speed to 3-5 seconds.

**Q: Why are my photos all white / overexposed?**
A: The environment is too bright or your ISO is too high. Find a darker place, set ISO to 100, and avoid shooting where there are streetlights or room lights on.

**Q: Why are my photos blurry?**
A: Your phone wasn't held still! The phone absolutely cannot move during shooting — use a tripod or prop it against something stable. Hand-held shots will always be blurry.

**Q: Why do I only see a light strip, not a pattern?**
A: You're moving too fast, or the light strip isn't facing the camera. Move slowly at a constant speed, and make sure the light strip faces your phone camera directly — don't angle it.

**Q: Why is the person in my shot blurry?**
A: The person moved during shooting. Keep all body parts still except the hand holding the light painting stick — stand firmly in place.

**Q: My iPhone doesn't have Night mode — what do I do?**
A: iPhone X and earlier models don't have Night mode. Download a third-party camera app (like ProCamera) and set 3-second long exposure to shoot.

**Q: My Android doesn't have a Light Painting mode — where is it?**
A: Switch to Pro mode, set shutter speed (S) to 3 seconds, and ISO to 100. It works the same way.

## 🔋 Device Questions

**Q: My device won't turn on?**
A: Charge for 30 minutes first — the battery might be dead. If it still won't turn on, press and hold the power button for 10 seconds to force a restart.

**Q: The lights don't turn on / some LEDs aren't working?**
A: First confirm the device is powered on and the battery is sufficient. If some LEDs aren't working, contact after-sales support.

**Q: My device won't charge?**
A: Try a different charger and cable. Use a standard 5V/1A charger — don't use fast chargers.

**Q: How long does the battery last?**
A: 2-3 hours of continuous use on a full charge. That's plenty for everyday use. Just turn it off when not in use.

**Q: Can I replace the battery?**
A: It's a built-in rechargeable battery — no replacement needed. Just charge via Type-C, same as your phone.

---

## 📱 App Connection Questions

**Q: My phone can't find the device's Bluetooth?**
A: First confirm the device is powered on and your phone's Bluetooth is on. Turn Bluetooth off and back on, or restart both the device and phone Bluetooth.

**Q: I can't connect to the app?**
A: Make sure the app is the latest version. Grant the app Bluetooth and location permissions — Android requires location permission to search for Bluetooth.

**Q: How do I upload my own images?**
A: Open the app and connect to the device, select image upload, and follow the on-screen instructions. Supports JPG/PNG formats.

**Q: My uploaded image doesn't display correctly?**
A: Don't use excessively high resolution images. The image width should ideally match the number of LEDs, and higher contrast gives better display results.

## ❓ Other Common Questions

**Q: Do I need a tripod?**
A: Not necessarily — anything that keeps your phone still works. Prop it against a wall, place it on a table, or weigh it down. The key is the phone doesn't move during shooting.

**Q: Do I need a professional camera?**
A: No — your phone works perfectly. All our tutorials are phone-based, and phones produce results good enough for any use case.

**Q: Can I shoot during the day?**
A: No. Light painting requires long exposure. Daytime is too bright and your shots will be all white. You must shoot at night or in a dark environment.

**Q: Can I connect it to a computer?**
A: Yes — developers can connect to a computer for coding and secondary development. Regular users will find the mobile app more than sufficient.

**Q: What if my device breaks?**
A: One-year warranty for non-man-made damage. Contact shop customer service for assistance.
