## 📦 Detailed Product Comparison

### 1. OpenStick T1 Entry Light Painting Stick (Best for Beginners)

- **Best for**: First-time users, budget-conscious, casual everyday shooting
- **Ready to use out of the box**: No assembly, no coding — power on and shoot
- **What you can do**: Write names, draw simple patterns, shoot holiday greetings, everything for casual light painting
- **Highlights**: Great value, simple to operate, the safest choice for first-time buyers
- **Limitations**: Fewer LEDs, complex patterns won't look as sharp as the S1, limited expandability

### 2. OpenStick S1 Pro Light Painting Stick (One-and-Done Choice)

- **Best for**: Serious light painting enthusiasts, creative work / portraits / commercial shoots
- **Ready to use out of the box**: Same out-of-box experience, power on and shoot
- **What you can do**: Complex high-resolution patterns, portrait light painting, creative work, commercial shoots
- **Highlights**: Denser LEDs, sharper display, higher brightness, more built-in effects, strong expandability — one device that covers everything
- **Limitations**: Higher price than the T1

### 3. Standalone Controllers (Firefly T1 / Rhino S1)

- **Best for**: DIY enthusiasts, coders, custom projects, using different light strips / boards
- **Note**: Standalone controllers do **not** include light strips or housing — you source your own accessories. Best for hands-on users.
- **What you can do**: Connect different lengths of light strips, create custom light painting devices, secondary development, other creative projects (pixel screens, ambient lights, inventions)
- **Difference**: Firefly T1 is the entry controller; Rhino S1 is the pro controller with stronger performance and expandability
- **Not recommended**: Pure beginners should not buy standalone controllers — get a complete kit instead for a hassle-free experience

### 4. LM Cam Dynamic Light Painting Host

- **Best for**: Dynamic light painting videos, more creative light painting content
- **Highlights**: Not a regular light painting stick — it's a dedicated device for dynamic light painting, capable of continuous dynamic light effects
- **What you can do**: Dynamic light painting videos, portrait dynamic light effects, creative short videos

## 🔧 How to Choose Accessories?

You don't need to buy everything at once. Choose accessories as needed:

1. **Different length light strips**: Get longer strips for larger text / patterns; the default length is enough for everyday use
2. **Light boards**: Useful for special-shaped light painting (hearts, stars) — not required
3. **Tripod**: Essential for light painting. If you don't have one, prop your phone against something — a tripod just makes it easier
4. **Extension rod**: Useful for large scenes / high-angle light painting — not required
5. **Motorized turntable**: For 360° rotating light painting — optional for advanced users

:::tip Beginner Tip
Just buy the light painting stick itself first. Get accessories later when you know what you actually need — don't buy a bunch of stuff upfront.
:::
