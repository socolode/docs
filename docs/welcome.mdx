## 💡 About Socolode

Socolode is an **AI-native open-source creative hardware brand**. We believe great hardware should be open, extensible, and combined with AI capabilities to make creative ideas accessible to everyone:

- **✅ AI-Native**: All hardware products natively support AI programming. Basic functions are built into the hardware, and AI enables real-time application features without complex development, significantly lowering the barrier to creativity
- **✅ Fully Open-Source**: Hardware schematics, firmware, and APIs are all open-source — no black boxes
- **✅ Beginner-Friendly**: Out of the box, no coding skills needed to create professional results
- **✅ Infinite Extensibility**: Supports custom programming, from light painting to various creative projects
- **✅ Continuous Updates**: Firmware is continuously iterated, and the community keeps producing new techniques and tutorials

### Brand Philosophy

- **Everyone is a creator** — Create art without a professional background
- **Technology serves creativity** — Lower the barriers to creation, unleash imagination
- **Community-driven innovation** — User feedback drives product progress
- **Sustainable development** — Eco-friendly materials, long-lasting use
