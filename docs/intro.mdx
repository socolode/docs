# Introduction

Welcome to the **Socolode** documentation. This is the single source of truth for everything you need to build, customize, and operate your Socolode store.

## What is Socolode?

Socolode is an e-commerce platform that lets you create and manage an independent online store. It handles product catalogs, checkout, payments, shipping, and storefront design, so you can focus on growing your business.

## Who is this documentation for?

- **Store owners** setting up their first online store
- **Developers** integrating with the Socolode API or customizing themes
- **Operations teams** managing orders, payments, and fulfillment
