# Install Your First App

Get your Beetle S1 glowing in 5 minutes. Follow the steps below to install and run your first app.

## Step 1: Download an App

Open the <Link to="../effects-projects/effect-list-install">App List & Installation</Link> page or the [official GitHub repository](https://github.com/socolode/beetle-s1), choose a light app you like and download it.

## Step 2: Upload to Beetle S1

Connect Beetle S1 to your computer via USB, then upload the downloaded app to Beetle S1.

## Step 3: Connect the LED Strip

Connect the LED strip correctly to the Beetle S1 port. For wiring instructions, see <Link to="../effects-projects/compatible-leds-wiring">Compatible LEDs & Wiring</Link>.

## Step 4: Enjoy the App

Power on and watch the downloaded app's colorful lights on your LED strip.

## Step 5: Learn to Create Your Own App

Want to define your own light apps? Head to <Link to="../../app-development/first-script">Write Your First App</Link> to learn how to build your own from scratch.
