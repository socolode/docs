# Beetle S1 - FAQ

## Power On & Off

### Q: Beetle S1 won't power on?
**A:** Check the following:
1. Is the battery charged? Try charging before powering on.
2. Hold the "center button" for 2+ seconds.
3. Try connecting to a charger to verify it's not a battery issue.

### Q: How to power off?
**A:** From the home screen (not in an effect), press the "down button" once.

### Q: Does it auto power off?
**A:** Yes. The S1 automatically powers off after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Charging & Power

### Q: How long to charge?
**A:** About 2 hours with a 5V/2A+ charger. The red LED on the charge port turns off when fully charged.

### Q: Can I use it while charging?
**A:** Yes. When plugged in, the device automatically switches to external power mode.

### Q: What's the difference between the two Type-C ports?
**A:** One is for fast charging only, the other is for data transfer (connecting to computer).

## Computer Connection

### Q: No USB drive appears when connected?
**A:** Ensure:
1. You're using the data port (not the charge port).
2. The USB cable supports data transfer.
3. The device is powered on.
4. Try a different USB port or cable.

### Q: Computer doesn't detect the device?
**A:** For firmware flashing mode, power off the device, hold the "right direction button", then connect to USB.

## Light Effects

### Q: How to install new effects?
**A:** See the <Link to="../effects-projects/effect-list-install">Effect List & Installation</Link> tutorial. Drag and drop via USB drive.

### Q: Effect not showing after adding?
**A:** Check if the `config.json` file was filled in correctly. See the effect installation tutorial for details.

### Q: How to delete effects?
**A:** Connect to computer, enter USB drive mode, delete the effect folder from `/apps/`, and remove the corresponding entry from `config.json`.

## LEDs & Wiring

### Q: What LED strips does the S1 support?
**A:** WS2812 and similar smart LED strips/rings via the XT30 connector. See the <Link to="../effects-projects/compatible-leds-wiring">Compatible LEDs & Wiring</Link> guide.

### Q: How to wire the XT30 connector?
**A:** The XT30(2+2) connector has 2 high-current power terminals and 2 control signal terminals. The S1 comes with an adapter cable for direct connection. See the wiring guide for details.

## Expansion & Programming

### Q: Are the S1 and T1 APIs compatible?
**A:** Mostly compatible. Both run the same MicroPython system with similar custom APIs. However, the S1 has no built-in microphone or 9-axis sensor, so effects relying on these won't work directly without external modules.

### Q: What can I connect to the 4PIN port?
**A:** The 4PIN port supports SPI/I2C/UART/GPIO for connecting sensor modules such as microphones, IMUs, displays, etc.
