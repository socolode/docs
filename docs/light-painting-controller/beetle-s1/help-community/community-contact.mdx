## Community Platform

The Discord community is the Beetle S1 user hub where you can:

- **Share your work**: Show off your light effects and creative projects
- **Exchange ideas**: Discuss techniques and tips with other creators
- **Get help**: Community members are happy to assist with issues
- **Join development**: Participate in firmware and effect development discussions

After joining Discord, check the #announcements and #getting-started channels for community rules and resources.

## Technical Support

If you encounter issues, seek help in this order:

1. Check the <Link to="./faq">FAQ</Link> first - most questions are answered there
2. Search or ask in the Discord community
3. Submit bug reports or feature requests via GitHub Issues
