# What can it do?

## Light Painting Photography

The Beetle S1's core use case. Use the light painting wand kit to create stunning light trails in the dark. The handle shape is comfortable to grip, making long creative sessions a breeze.

**You need:** Beetle S1 + Light Painting Wand Kit + Tripod + Camera/phone with long exposure

See the <Link to="../effects-projects/light-painting-wand">Light Painting Wand Kit</Link> guide for details.

## Desktop Ambient Light

Attach an LED ring and the Beetle S1 becomes a desktop ambient light. Gradient, breathing, rhythm effects - the most eye-catching decoration on your desk.

**You need:** Beetle S1 + LED ring

## Handheld Light Show

The handle shape makes it a great portable light show. Perfect for parties, music festivals, camping - wave it in your hand to create atmosphere.

**You need:** Beetle S1 + LED strip

## Large Light Installations

High power output can drive longer LED strips, ideal for event lighting, party decoration, and outdoor art installations.

**You need:** Beetle S1 + multiple LED strips

## Interactive Light Art

Connect external sensors via the 4PIN expansion port to create interactive light installations - sound-reactive lights, gesture-controlled effects, smart感应 lights, and more.

**You need:** Beetle S1 + sensor module + 4PIN wiring

See the <Link to="../effects-projects/4pin-expansion">4PIN Expansion Interface</Link> guide for details.

## And More...

This is just the beginning. Combine with 3D-printed enclosures and open-source programming to create even more. The Beetle S1's possibilities are limited only by your imagination.
