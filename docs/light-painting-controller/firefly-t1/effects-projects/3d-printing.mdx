## Printing Tips

- **Material**: PLA or PETG recommended
- **Layer height**: 0.2mm for good results
- **Supports**: May be needed depending on model geometry
- **Infill**: 15%-20% is sufficient

## Assembly Guide

1. Clean support material and rough edges after printing
2. Place Firefly T1 into the enclosure, aligning the ports
3. Secure with screws if included
4. Connect LED strip/ring and enjoy
