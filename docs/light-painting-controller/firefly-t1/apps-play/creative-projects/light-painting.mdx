# Light Painting Wand

Assemble your Firefly T1 with an LED strip, a light stick, and a dedicated base to create a light painting wand, letting you paint light trails in the night sky for stunning photos.

## What You Need

- Firefly T1 main unit
- LED strip
- Light painting wand base (available in the <Link to="../3d-printing">3D printing files</Link>)
- Light stick (can be DIY)

## Preparation

- Assembled light painting wand
- Camera or phone
- Tripod
- Find a completely dark or low-light environment

## FAQ

### Trails not bright enough?
- Increase LED brightness
- Use higher density strip (144/m)
- Slow down your movement

### Background too bright?
- Lower ISO
- Narrow aperture (increase f value)
- Choose a darker environment
