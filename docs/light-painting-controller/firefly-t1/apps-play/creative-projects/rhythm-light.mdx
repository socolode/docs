# Desktop Rhythm Light

The LED ring dances to music, changing colors and brightness based on audio frequency response — the most eye-catching ambient light for your desk.

## What You Need

- Firefly T1 main unit
- WS2812 LED ring (24+ LEDs recommended)
- Audio source (phone, computer, or microphone)

## Preparation

- Follow the <Link to="../compatible-leds-wiring">wiring guide</Link> to connect the LED ring
- Install the rhythm light effect file
- Place the ring near the audio source

## Usage

### Audio Response Mode

The ring responds to ambient audio frequency and intensity in real-time. Low frequencies (bass) appear as warm colors, high frequencies as cool colors.

### Custom Mode

Manually adjust sensitivity, color scheme, and brightness range to match different music styles.

## Placement Tips

- **Desktop**: Next to your monitor, USB powered
- **Party**: Combine multiple rings for a party atmosphere
- **Streaming**: As a background ambient light for visual effect

## Creative Ideas

- Combine different colored rings
- Pair with a <Link to="../3d-printing">3D printed enclosure</Link> for a diffused light effect
