# Highlights & Features

## Compact & Portable

Palm-sized, fits in your pocket. Whether for outdoor light painting, desktop decoration, or party lighting, it's ready whenever you are.

- Powered by built-in 18650 Li-ion battery, no cable tethering
- Type-C charging, share cables with your phone
- Lightweight design, approximately XX grams

## Plug & Play

No soldering, no programming required. Install the battery and it's ready to go. Plug into your computer to transfer files. Get your first light effect running in 5 minutes.

- Drag-and-drop effect installation via USB drive mode
- Five-way joystick for quick effect switching
- Supports LED strips and rings

## Open-Source & Programmable

Know Python? Write your own light effects. Built on MicroPython, easy to learn with a rich community ecosystem.

- Complete custom API
- MicroPython standard library support
- Open-source firmware with regular updates

## Unlimited Creativity

From light painting photography to desktop clocks, from rhythm lights to interactive installations — your imagination is the only limit.

- Compatible with common WS2812 LED strips and rings
- 3D printable enclosure for personalized looks
- Community-driven effects and project sharing
