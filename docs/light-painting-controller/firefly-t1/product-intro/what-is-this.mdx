# Firefly T1 Product Introduction

## What Is Firefly T1?

Firefly T1 is a portable, programmable light controller designed specifically for exploring colorful LED lights. It comes with a variety of mainstream sensors, a replaceable 18650 battery, and rich expansion interfaces. Combined with AI programming, both photography enthusiasts and electronics hobbyists can easily get started and craft any lighting effect you need.

## Why Use This Device?

You've probably seen others capture cool light painting photos or turn their desk lighting into something fun, and felt like "that's only for people who know what they're doing." Firefly T1 is built to solve exactly that "want to play but afraid of the hassle" pain point — it turns complex lighting creation into something you can just switch on and play.

- **Capture ideas on the fly**: No need to study hardware — install the battery, attach a light wand, pair with your camera, and shoot unique light painting shots
- **One device for every occasion**: Photography, desk ambiance, parties, live streaming — switch to the right effect for any moment
- **Make something truly unique**: Not satisfied with cookie-cutter decor lights? Customize to your own taste and create effects no one else has
- **It only gets more fun**: Start with ready-made effects, then go deeper as you learn — the possibilities never run out

## Built-in Configuration

The Firefly T1 host integrates a rich set of sensors and components, ready to play out of the box:

- **Microphone**: captures sound so the lights pulse with music or ambient audio
- **9-axis sensor**: detects motion and orientation — wave or shake to trigger effects
- **Buzzer**: emits tones for sound-and-light feedback
- **Color screen**: 1.14" color display, effects and status at a glance
- **5-way joystick**: select, confirm, and back with one hand — easy operation
- **5P mini connector**: enables ultra-narrow board-to-board splicing for more flexible accessory design

## Highlights

### Compact & Portable

- Powered by built-in 18650 Li-ion battery, no cable tethering
- Type-C charging, share cables with your phone
- Barely heavier than a single 18650 battery

### Plug & Play

- Drag-and-drop effect installation via USB drive mode
- Five-way joystick for quick effect switching
- Supports LED strips and rings

### Open-Source & Programmable

- Complete custom API
- Built on MicroPython, rich community ecosystem
- Open-source firmware with regular updates

### Unlimited Creativity
