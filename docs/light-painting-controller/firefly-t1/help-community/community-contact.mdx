## Community Platform

The Discord community is the home base for Firefly T1 users. Here you can:

- **Share your work**: Showcase your light effects and creative projects
- **Exchange ideas**: Share tips and experiences with other creators
- **Get help**: Have community members help answer your questions
- **Join development**: Participate in firmware and effect development discussions

When you join Discord, check the #announcements and #getting-started channels first for community rules and resource indexes.

## Technical Support

If you encounter issues while using the product:

1. Check the <Link to="./faq">FAQ</Link> first — most questions are already answered
2. Search or ask on the Discord community — fellow users usually respond quickly
3. Submit issues via GitHub Issues — developers monitor regularly
